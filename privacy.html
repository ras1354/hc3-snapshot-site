HC3 Snapshot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Purpose

HC3 Snapshot is a personal application designed to back up configuration data from a Fibaro Home Center 3 controller.

Information Collected

The application retrieves scene definitions, Lua source code, QuickApp configurations and device information from the owner's Home Center 3 controller.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 with the drive.file permission to create and manage its own snapshot files in the owner's Google Drive.

Data Storage

Snapshots are stored locally on the owner's Raspberry Pi and uploaded to the owner's private Google Drive. Snapshot contents are not published on this website.

The snapshots may contain sensitive configuration information and are not individually encrypted by the application.

Data Sharing

The application is intended exclusively for personal use. It does not sell data or intentionally share snapshot contents with other users.

Retention and Deletion

Snapshots remain stored until the owner deletes them. Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about the application can be directed to the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
